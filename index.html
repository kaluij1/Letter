To My Friend

Five years of university.
Thousands of moments.

Where It Started

We started as strangers in a crowded lecture hall, two faces among hundreds. I never imagined that a simple "Is this seat taken?" would lead to five years of shared laughter, late-night study sessions, and countless coffee runs. Looking back, that first conversation was the beginning of something extraordinary.

The Ordinary Days

It wasn't the big moments that defined our friendship—it was the ordinary ones. The Tuesday afternoons at our favorite café. The walks across campus debating everything from philosophy to pizza toppings. The comfortable silences between lectures. These simple, unremarkable days became the foundation of something beautiful and lasting.

The Difficult Seasons

We weathered storms together—exam stress, family troubles, heartbreaks, and doubts. In those moments when everything felt overwhelming, you were there. Not with grand gestures or perfect advice, but with presence. With understanding. With a reminder that I wasn't alone. Those difficult seasons taught me that true friendship isn't just about sharing joy—it's about sharing the weight of life's challenges.

Graduation

As we threw our caps in the air, I realized this wasn't an ending—it was a transformation. We're no longer the nervous freshmen who bonded over shared confusion. We've grown, changed, and discovered who we are. And through it all, our friendship has been the constant. Whatever comes next, wherever life takes us, these five years have given me something irreplaceable: a friend for life.

We came here for degrees.
Somehow I leave with stories.

Thank you.
